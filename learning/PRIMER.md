# A worked route from noisy states to the channel theorem

[Learning entrypoint](README.md) · [Source bridge](HOLEVO_BRIDGE.md) · [Milestones](MILESTONES.md)

A channel can retain a trace of entanglement without being a good memory. To make that distinction concrete, first consider two qubits. Then replace the noisy qubit by the project's continuous-variable carrier. The two-qubit calculation is an established illustrative example, not a new result of this repository.

## States, references and error

A density operator is positive and has trace one. A measurement effect $`0\le M\le I`$ has probability $`\mathrm{Tr}(M\rho)`$. For two states, half trace distance

```math
T(\rho,\sigma)=\tfrac12\|\rho-\sigma\|_1
```

bounds the difference in every such probability. For two channels, half diamond distance takes the largest corresponding distinguishability over inputs jointly prepared with an untouched reference. The reference detects correlations that inspecting the carrier alone can miss.

An **entanglement-breaking (EB)** channel produces a separable joint output for every input and every reference. It can be represented by measurement followed by state preparation. EB is a property of an entire channel, not of a chosen input. A **negative partial transpose (NPT)** output certifies entanglement: transposition on just the reference cannot turn a convex mixture of product states into an operator with a negative expectation. The partial transpose is a mathematical test, not a physical operation performed on an unknown state.

These objects connect Holevo §§2.4, 3.1 and 6.2–6.4 to the [infinite-dimensional definitions](../science/model.md). The project uses normal trace-class states and no energy restriction in its channel norm. Explicit witnesses nevertheless have finite preparation energy.

## Worked example: a qubit can be almost classical without being EB

Let $`Z=\operatorname{diag}(1,-1)`$ and, for $`0\le q\le1`$, define

```math
\mathcal E_q(\rho)=\frac{1+q}{2}\rho+\frac{1-q}{2}Z\rho Z.
```

Populations are unchanged and off-diagonal entries are multiplied by $`q`$. Send one half of the Bell state $`(|00\rangle+|11\rangle)/\sqrt2`$ through the channel. In the ordered basis $`00,01,10,11`$, the output is

```math
\rho_q=\frac12\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&0&q\\
0&0&0&0\\
0&0&0&0\\
q&0&0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
```

Its reference partial transpose has eigenvalues $`1/2,1/2,q/2,-q/2`$. Thus every $`q>0`$ leaves an entangled output. At $`q=0`$, the channel is complete dephasing $`\Delta`$, which measures the computational basis and prepares the observed basis state, so it is EB.

The distance to the whole EB set is exactly

```math
\inf_{\Psi\in\mathrm{EB}}\tfrac12\|\mathcal E_q-\Psi\|_\diamond=q/2.
```

For the upper bound, $`\mathcal E_q-\Delta=(q/2)(\mathrm{id}-\operatorname{Ad}_Z)`$, and the diamond distance between identity and the phase-flip channel is two: their outputs on $`|+\rangle`$ are orthogonal, attaining the maximum. For the lower bound, let $`|v\rangle=(|01\rangle-|10\rangle)/\sqrt2`$ and $`W=(|v\rangle\langle v|)^{\Gamma_R}`$. Then $`\|W\|_\infty=1/2`$, $`\mathrm{Tr}(W\rho_q)=-q/2`$, and its expectation on every separable state is nonnegative. The trace-norm inequality forces every EB comparator to be at least $`q/2`$ away on the Bell input.

Taking $`q=e^{-\gamma t}`$ with $`\gamma>0`$ gives no finite EB time, but exponentially small distance to EB. This simple example shows why the generic distinction is not the new discovery. The project's contribution is its **specific nonlinear threshold, singular Gaussian comparison and cubic logarithmic decay law**.

## From the example to a random shear

The carrier now has position and momentum satisfying $`[Q,P]=i`$. Free evolution generated by $`H=P^2/2`$ produces a shear:

```math
U_s=e^{-isH},\qquad U_s^\dagger Q U_s=Q+sP.
```

The project combines three unobserved noises: Q-generated momentum kicks at strength $`D`$, kinetic-energy dephasing at strength $`\kappa`$, and P-generated position kicks at strength $`\eta`$. With $`D,\kappa>0`$, the kinetic noise changes the deterministic shear to

```math
S_s=\omega s+\sqrt{2\kappa}B_s.
```

Here $`B_s`$ is a standard Brownian motion, whose increment over a duration $`ds`$ has variance $`ds`$. Holding this trajectory fixed for analysis, the other kicks remain Gaussian. Their covariance in the interaction picture is

```math
V_S=2D\begin{pmatrix}
\int_0^tS_s^2ds&-\int_0^tS_sds\\
-\int_0^tS_sds&t
\end{pmatrix}
+2\eta t\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
```

This is classical displacement covariance, not state covariance. The vacuum state has covariance $`I_2/2`$, and a one-mode additive Gaussian channel is EB once $`\sqrt{\det V_S}\ge1`$ in these units. Holevo §§12.2–12.4 and §12.6 provide the Gaussian foundations; the [conditional derivation](../science/conditional_gaussian.md) supplies the stochastic step.

Writing $`\overline S=t^{-1}\int_0^tS_sds`$ gives

```math
\det V_S=4D\eta t^2+4D^2t\int_0^t(S_s-\overline S)^2ds.
```

The first term is a path-independent floor; the second measures temporal variation of the shear. If $`4D\eta t^2\ge1`$, all components are EB and their average is EB. The endpoint is included. No observer actually learns $`S`$ or corrects it for free.

## Why the converse needs a new argument

Before the floor reaches one, some conditional channels are not EB. That fact alone is insufficient: mixtures can erase the very entanglement each component preserves. A familiar example is the equal mixture of identity and phase flip above, which yields the EB channel $`\Delta`$.

The project instead prepares normalized momentum wave packets centered at $`+A`$ and $`-A`$, correlated with orthogonal reference labels. The output witness compares two quantities: useful off-diagonal coherence $`c_A`$ and wrong-packet population $`d_A`$. Its partial-transpose expectation is $`(d_A-c_A)/2`$. At width $`\delta=1/A`$, analytical bounds give a logarithmic advantage whose dominant terms are

```math
\left(\frac1{Dt}-4\eta t\right)A^2
-2\sqrt{D\kappa}\,tA+O(\log A).
```

Strictly below $`t=(2\sqrt{D\eta})^{-1}`$, the quadratic coefficient is positive. A sufficiently large **finite** separation therefore gives NPT. When $`\eta=0`$, this works at every finite time. The [full proof](../science/exact_threshold.md) retains normalization, drift damping and finite preparation cost; the expression here explains the comparison rather than replacing the proof.

## A complete parameter comparison

In dimensionless units take $`D=1`$, $`\eta=1/4`$, $`\omega=1`$. For every positive $`\kappa`$, the exact EB time is $`1`$. At $`\kappa=0`$ the total channel is Gaussian and the separate determinant is $`t^2+t^4/3`$. Its EB time is

```math
t_G=\sqrt{\frac{\sqrt{21}-3}{2}}\approx0.88954.
```

The positive-$`\kappa`$ result cannot be obtained by substituting zero into a proof that requires a positive harmonic kernel. This is a discontinuity of the exact EB boundary, not a promise that adding noise improves useful memory. The [Gaussian comparison](../science/interpretation.md) also bounds how small the operational distinction can be.

## Why the operational error has cubic logarithmic order

Set $`\eta=0`$. Brownian scaling converts the variance term into $`8D^2\kappa t^3Z`$, where $`Z`$ is a nonnegative integrated square of a centered Brownian path with an additional deterministic drift. Most long-time paths produce an EB conditional channel. Replace the exceptional paths by state preparation and keep all the good paths: the resulting channel is EB and its half-diamond error is at most the exceptional probability, uniformly over every input and reference.

The exact Laplace transform of $`Z`$ yields a cubic-exponential upper bound. Separately, orthogonal compact packets yield a lower bound on the **same** metric. In canonical units, the proved bounds have cubic coefficients one and four; these constants are not matched. Thus the conclusion is $`-\log e(t)=\Theta(D^2\kappa t^3)`$, not a claimed exact leading exponent. See [cubic distance](../science/cubic_distance.md).

## What survives, and what does not follow

Every quantitative packet used above has finite energy, but this chosen construction's energy grows with time. A different argument shows that one fixed finitely squeezed two-mode input remains NPT forever when $`\eta=0`$: finite-rank local filtering on the reference would reveal the time-dependent witness, contradicting PPT if the original output were PPT. The filter can have negligible success probability. The [fixed-input account](../science/fixed_input.md) proves this precise statement.

Meanwhile any deterministic encoding and final recovery of a logical $`d`$-level system, with no quantum bypass, obey

```math
F_e\le\frac1d+e(t).
```

An EB comparator has maximally entangled overlap at most $`1/d`$, and channel-distance contractivity bounds the additional overlap. This is a one-use statement, not an infinite-dimensional capacity formula. The result can therefore preserve exact NPT while ruling out high-fidelity logical storage at late times in this model.
