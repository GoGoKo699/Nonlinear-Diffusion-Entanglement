# Scientific account: from non-Gaussian motion to channel classicalization

The full story starts from a known exactly solvable non-Gaussian diffusion, not from a new master equation. The central question is **whether decoherence destroys every form of reference entanglement after a finite time**, and how to compare that binary event with a small *operational* error for all inputs.

1. [Model](model.md) defines the normal random-unitary channel, stochastic parameters, exact entanglement-breaking and PPT notions, and charged input/reference resources.
2. [Conditional Gaussian representation](conditional_gaussian.md) derives the non-Gaussian mixture and its Brownian Gram determinant. It supplies a pathwise EB criterion and a channel-norm comparison without pretending that the unconditional channel is Gaussian.
3. [Exact threshold](exact_threshold.md) supplies both halves: a pathwise measure-and-prepare upper theorem and a finite-energy momentum-packet NPT witness matching it strictly below the boundary. The known Mehler heat kernel is reconstructed with the precise coefficients required here.
4. [Cubic proximity](cubic_distance.md) establishes upper and lower bounds of cubic logarithmic order when $`\eta=0`$; the lower witness and the reference-uniform upper bound concern the *same* unconstrained channel norm.
5. [Fixed input](fixed_input.md) shows why one full-Schmidt-rank, finite-energy two-mode squeezed state already detects qualitative non-PPT dynamics at all finite times, and separates that result from quantitative survival.
6. [Interpretation](interpretation.md) compares the deterministic-shear Gaussian limit, the singular weak-quadratic-noise transition, and the limits of memory or capacity inferences.

[Primary sources and contribution boundaries](../references/RELATED_WORK.md) identify established methods. [Reproduction](../verification/README.md) checks the numerical identities. The theorem equations are canonical in the topic pages; a test result is not substituted for a proof.
